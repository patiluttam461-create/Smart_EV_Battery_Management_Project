## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Objective

In standard machine learning evaluations, models are often evaluated on held-out cycles from the **same** physical battery cell (e.g., chronological split of Battery 1 into 371 train, 93 validation, and 116 test cycles). While this verifies temporal generalization along a single degradation path, it does **not** evaluate **cross-cell generalization**—whether an ANFIS model trained on Battery 1 can accurately estimate State of Health (SOH) for an unseen sister cell (Battery 2) subjected to manufacturing tolerances, cell-to-cell impedance variations, and independent degradation dynamics.

This document establishes the verified scientific protocol, data acquisition pathway, preprocessing pipeline, and evaluation script to benchmark First-Order Sugeno ANFIS on **Samsung INR18650-35E Battery 2** under rigorous academic standards:

1. **Zero Data Leakage:** Preprocessing scalers and model parameters are fitted **strictly** on Battery 1 training cycles (1–371). Battery 2 data is never seen during training or tuning.
2. **Canonical Schema Parity:** Enforces identical 7 health indicator features, feature ordering, and engineering transformations.
3. **Naive Baseline Benchmarking:** Benchmarks ANFIS against Train-Mean, Persistence (Final B1 Cycle and Initial B2 State), and Linear Trend aging extrapolation.
4. **Validation-Only Bias Calibration:** Evaluates whether systematic bias calibrated purely on Battery 1 validation residuals ($\bar{e}_{\text{val}}$) transfers to Battery 2, without test label snooping.
5. **Academic Integrity Protection:** Strictly prohibits fabricating or interpolating raw measurement files. If Battery 2 raw telemetry is not present on disk, the pipeline halts with a diagnostic report.

## 5. Cross-Battery Evaluation Protocol

To run the cross-battery evaluation, execute:

```bash
python -m src.evaluation.evaluate_cross_battery
```

### Scientific Isolation Guarantees

1. **Scaler Isolation:**
   $$\mu_{\text{scaler}} = \frac{1}{371} \sum_{i=1}^{371} X_{\text{B1, train}, i}, \quad \sigma_{\text{scaler}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{371} \sum_{i=1}^{371} (X_{\text{B1, train}, i} - \mu)^2}$$
   This scaler transforms Battery 2 features:
   $$X_{\text{B2, scaled}} = \frac{X_{\text{B2}} - \mu_{\text{scaler}}}{\sigma_{\text{scaler}}}$$
   Battery 2 is never included in mean or variance computations.

2. **Model Training Isolation:**
   An experimental First-Order Sugeno ANFIS ($R=2$, $\lambda=5.0$, $\eta=0.005$) is trained strictly on Battery 1 training cycles, with early stopping monitored on Battery 1 validation cycles. Production model checkpoints (`models/anfis_first_order_model.joblib`) are completely isolated and untouched.

3. **Validation-Only Bias Calibration:**
   During Battery 1 evaluation, ANFIS exhibited a systematic positive offset ($+0.8246\%$). In `evaluate_cross_battery.py`, the validation bias is estimated **strictly** from Battery 1 validation residuals:
   $$\bar{e}_{\text{val}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{val}}} \sum_{j=1}^{N_{\text{val}}} (\hat{y}_{\text{val}, j} - y_{\text{val}, j})$$
   The validation-calibrated prediction for Battery 2 is:
   $$\hat{y}_{\text{B2, val\_cal}} = \hat{y}_{\text{B2, uncal}} - \bar{e}_{\text{val}}$$
   This rigorously tests whether the calibration offset reflects a general sensor/model characteristic or cell-specific idiosyncrasy.

## 6. Naive Baseline Models for Cross-Battery Benchmarking

To prove whether the ANFIS model learns genuine degradation dynamics across cells, `evaluate_cross_battery.py` compares it against four standard operational baselines:

1. **Train-Mean Baseline:**
   $$\hat{y}_i = \frac{1}{371} \sum_{j=1}^{371} y_{\text{B1, train}, j}$$
   Tests whether ANFIS outperforms a constant prediction equal to Battery 1's mean training health.

2. **Persistence Baseline (Final B1 Train Cycle):**
   $$\hat{y}_i = y_{\text{B1, train}}[371]$$
   Tests whether ANFIS outperforms assuming the cell remains at the final known health of the training battery.

3. **Persistence Baseline (Initial B2 State):**
   $$\hat{y}_i = y_{\text{B2}}[1]$$
   Tests whether assuming an unseen battery maintains its initial fresh capacity throughout its lifetime beats model predictions.

4. **Linear Trend Extrapolation Baseline:**
   Fit ordinary least squares (OLS) linear decay on Battery 1 training cycles:
   $$SOH = m \cdot CN + c$$
   and extrapolate across Battery 2 cycle numbers:
   $$\hat{y}_i = m \cdot CN_i + c$$

## 9. Academic Interpretation Guide for Defense & Review

When Battery 2 raw files are acquired and evaluation is executed, interpret the results using the following academic criteria:

1. **Cross-Cell Error vs Within-Cell Error:**
   - In Battery 1 held-out test evaluation, First-Order ANFIS achieved **MAE 0.8246%** and **RMSE 0.8574%**.
   - An increase in error on Battery 2 is scientifically expected due to cell-to-cell variability (impedance, active material mass differences, electrode thickness variation).
   - If Battery 2 MAE is $< 2.0\%$, the neuro-fuzzy system demonstrates strong cross-cell transferability.

2. **Benchmarking Against Naive Baselines:**
   - If ANFIS achieves lower RMSE and MAE than the **Train-Mean**, **Persistence**, and **Linear Trend** baselines, it proves that the model extracts genuine physical degradation signals rather than memorizing a fixed decay schedule.

3. **Validation Bias Calibration Efficacy:**
   - If $\text{MAE}(\hat{y}_{\text{val\_cal}}) < \text{MAE}(\hat{y}_{\text{uncal}})$, the systematic positive offset observed in Battery 1 is a cell-independent feature of the ANFIS architecture.
   - If uncalibrated performs better or calibration increases error, the offset was specific to Battery 1's impedance trajectory.
